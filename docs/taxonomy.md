## Financial technology capabilities

### Market Data
Acquisition, REST, WebSocket, normalization, ticks, OHLCV, order books, instrument masters, corporate actions, historical storage, data quality.

### Execution
OMS, EMS, order state, routing, smart order routing, fills, slippage, retry, idempotency, reconciliation, broker abstraction.

### Research
Backtesting, simulation, portfolio construction, factor research, optimization, feature engineering, experiment management.

### Risk
Pre-trade checks, exposure, limits, margin, drawdown, stress testing, scenario analysis, kill switches.

### Platform
Configuration, APIs, persistence, event systems, observability, deployment, recovery, security, developer experience.

## Cross-cutting intelligence

Requirements, interfaces/contracts, data models, temporal correctness, state management, testing, reproducibility, migrations, cost, threat modeling, compliance, human factors, community health, project failure, and technology evolution.

## Indian-market first-class scope

NSE, BSE, MCX, Indian equities, futures/options, broker APIs, market hours, instrument masters, expiries, corporate actions, brokerage and statutory charges, slippage, WebSockets, reconciliation, and relevant SEBI/RBI/exchange requirements.
