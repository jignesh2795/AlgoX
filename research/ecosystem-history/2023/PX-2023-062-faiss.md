# PX-2023-062 — FAISS

## Classification
- Year: 2023
- Domain: retrieval / vector similarity
- Research status: discovery record
- Evidence maturity: C1

## Historical relevance
FAISS provided mature high-performance similarity-search primitives for dense vectors and became a foundational component in semantic retrieval systems.

## Capability extraction
- approximate nearest-neighbor search
- exact similarity search
- vector indexing
- configurable speed/memory trade-offs
- large-scale embedding retrieval

## AlgoX relevance
FAISS reinforces the distinction between a retrieval index and institutional memory. A vector index can accelerate candidate retrieval but should not become the authoritative representation of evidence, provenance or temporal state.

## Decision
ADOPT the architectural lesson; HOLD FAISS as a direct dependency until retrieval benchmarks justify it.
