# PX-2023-025 — vLLM / PagedAttention

## Historical role
The vLLM work was published in September 2023 with PagedAttention, an approach inspired by virtual-memory paging for managing LLM KV-cache memory. The paper reported substantially higher throughput than contemporary serving systems under comparable latency. citeturn1academia13

## Architecture observations
- LLM serving became a systems problem involving memory fragmentation, batching, cache sharing, and scheduling.
- Model quality alone does not determine application performance; inference infrastructure materially affects throughput and cost.
- Operating-system concepts can transfer directly into ML systems design.
- Serving should be treated as an independently benchmarkable layer beneath applications and agents.

## Research value
Very high for local-model and production-inference architecture.

## AlgoX extraction
**Potential capability:** high-throughput model-serving abstraction.

**Lesson:** AlgoX should keep model-serving concerns below the research/agent layer and measure latency, throughput, memory, and cost independently from reasoning quality.

## Evidence
- vLLM/PagedAttention paper, September 2023. citeturn1academia13

## Decision status
ASSESS — include vLLM-class serving systems in the technology radar and later serving benchmarks; do not hard-code the architecture around one inference engine.

## Evidence maturity
C2 — published systems research; no AlgoX benchmark yet.
