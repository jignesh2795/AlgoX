# PX-2023-030 — FinRL

## Historical role
FinRL is an open-source framework for applying deep reinforcement learning to automated trading and financial decision-making. Its ecosystem includes market environments, data processing, DRL agents, trading applications, and benchmark-oriented workflows. It predates 2023, but its continued development makes it a useful historical reference for the 2023 AI+finance convergence.

## Architecture observations
- Trading research is modeled as an environment/agent/reward loop rather than as a single strategy function.
- Market data, environment simulation, learning algorithms and trading applications are separable concerns.
- Reinforcement-learning results are highly dependent on environment design, transaction costs, data quality and reward specification.

## AlgoX extraction
**Capability:** experiment-driven AI trading environment.

**Lesson:** AlgoX must never treat a reported trading-model result as portable without preserving market regime, dataset, transaction-cost assumptions, reward definition, execution model and evaluation protocol.
