# PX-2023-038 — QuantConnect LEAN

## Historical role
LEAN is an event-driven, modular algorithmic-trading engine designed to support research, backtesting and live execution. Its architecture separates algorithm logic from brokerage integrations and models asset classes, portfolio construction, risk and execution. ## Historical relevance
LEAN predates the 2023 AI wave. It is included because AlgoX needs mature trading-engine reference architectures alongside newer AI systems.

## Architecture observations
- Event-driven execution provides a common model for backtest and live operation.
- Brokerage plugins isolate venue-specific connectivity.
- Data normalization and corporate-action handling are explicit concerns.
- India support exposes market, brokerage, data and order-model differences rather than pretending markets are interchangeable.

## AlgoX extraction
**Capability:** event-driven multi-asset trading engine with pluggable brokerage adapters.

**Lesson:** a trading engine needs deterministic domain models underneath any AI layer. AI should advise/research/optimize within explicit portfolio, risk, execution and brokerage contracts.
