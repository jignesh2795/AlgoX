# PX-2023-080 — Indian Market 2023 Discovery Gap Audit

## Current conclusion
The 2023 Indian-market open-source ecosystem is now sufficiently represented to identify the major capability families, but it is not yet exhaustive at repository level.

## Confirmed families
- broker APIs and SDKs
- NSE/BSE historical-data acquisition
- bhavcopy/download tooling
- live quote and WebSocket access
- options/derivatives data utilities
- basic strategy/backtesting projects
- exchange/corporate-information utilities

## Still requiring targeted historical screening
- instrument-master/security-master projects
- corporate-action adjustment engines
- tick/order-book storage systems
- execution simulators and fill models
- broker reconciliation implementations
- margin/charges/tax calculators
- Indian F&O expiry/rollover utilities
- multi-broker normalization projects that demonstrably existed in 2023

## Important temporal rule
Current projects must not be backdated merely because they operate on NSE/BSE today. Historical commits, releases, package publication dates, archived documentation or contemporaneous evidence are required.

## Discovery assessment
2023 should remain open for targeted Indian-market discovery, but the research can now proceed to a capability-gap audit rather than endlessly enumerating small repositories.

## Confidence
Medium-high.
