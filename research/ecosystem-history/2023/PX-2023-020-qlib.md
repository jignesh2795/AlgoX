# PX-2023-020 — Microsoft Qlib

## Historical role
Qlib is an open-source, AI-oriented quantitative investment platform designed to cover quantitative research from data processing and model training through backtesting and production-oriented workflows. Its architecture uses loosely coupled modules and includes data infrastructure, model training, backtesting, alpha research, risk modeling, portfolio optimization, and execution-related components. citeturn1search0turn1search3

## Historical relevance
Qlib predates 2023, so this record is not evidence that it originated in the 2023 AI wave. It is included because the 2023 research pass must connect the AI ecosystem to the financial/quant ecosystem and identify mature systems that already solved parts of the target problem.

## Architecture observations
- Quant research is treated as a pipeline rather than an isolated strategy script.
- Data, training, evaluation, and backtesting are modularized.
- The platform spans research and production-oriented concerns, allowing AlgoX to compare AI-agent architecture with established quantitative-system architecture.
- Loose coupling makes individual components candidates for independent study rather than requiring adoption of the whole platform.

## Research value
Very high for AlgoX because it provides a financial-domain reference architecture against which generic AI research can be evaluated.

## AlgoX extraction
**Potential capability:** end-to-end quantitative research pipeline.

**Lesson:** AlgoX should not become an AI-project catalog disconnected from domain architecture. Financial research requires data lineage, backtesting, modeling, risk, portfolio construction, execution, and production boundaries.
