# PX-2023-021 — LLaMA

## Historical role
Meta introduced LLaMA in February 2023 as a family of foundation language models ranging from 7B to 65B parameters, emphasizing that smaller models could achieve strong results while requiring substantially less compute and enabling broader research access. citeturn3search4turn3search6

## Architecture observations
- Capability became less dependent on having access to the largest proprietary model.
- Smaller open/research-accessible models created a new local experimentation path.
- Model size became an explicit engineering variable alongside quality, latency, memory, and infrastructure cost.

## Research value
Very high because the shift toward smaller, locally deployable foundation models directly affected later local inference, coding agents, private research systems, and model-serving infrastructure.

## AlgoX extraction
**Potential capability:** model-provider and model-size abstraction.

**Lesson:** AlgoX should design AI-dependent components so that the underlying model can be replaced or down-sized without losing institutional knowledge or deterministic domain logic.

## Evidence
- Meta's February 2023 research publication. citeturn3search4
- Meta's announcement emphasizing lower compute requirements and research accessibility. citeturn3search6

## Decision status
