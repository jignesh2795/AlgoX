# PX-2023-065 — Axolotl

## Classification
- Year: 2023
- Domain: model post-training / fine-tuning
- Research status: discovery record
- Evidence maturity: C1

## Historical relevance
Axolotl emerged as an open-source configuration-driven approach to fine-tuning and post-training open language models, reducing the engineering barrier between datasets, training configuration, evaluation and model output.

## Capability extraction
- reproducible training configuration
- dataset preprocessing
- fine-tuning workflows
- LoRA/parameter-efficient training
- evaluation integration
- local/open-model experimentation

## AlgoX relevance
Useful for future experiments where AlgoX evaluates specialized research models. Training configuration, dataset provenance and evaluation results should be recorded as experiment evidence rather than hidden inside model artifacts.

## Decision
ASSESS — use as an experiment candidate, not as a core AlgoX dependency.
