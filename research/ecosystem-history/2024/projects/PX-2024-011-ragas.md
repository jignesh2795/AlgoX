# PX-2024-011 — RAGAs

**Repository:** https://github.com/explodinggradients/ragas
**Historical category:** RAG evaluation infrastructure
**Domain:** Retrieval-augmented generation evaluation

## 2024 signal
RAGAs established a reference-free evaluation approach for RAG pipelines, separating retrieval quality, context use/faithfulness, and generation quality.

## Capability observed
RAG should be evaluated as a pipeline rather than as one model output. Retrieval can fail even when generation appears fluent; generation can also fail despite relevant retrieval.

## Architectural lesson
AlgoX's evidence retrieval must expose measurable retrieval and synthesis quality instead of treating retrieved context as automatically trustworthy.

## AlgoX extraction
- Retrieval evaluation
- Faithfulness evaluation
- Context relevance
- Reference-free evaluation
- Component-level quality signals

## Relevance to AlgoX
High. Research answers need retrieval-quality and evidence-grounding measurements before findings are promoted into institutional knowledge.
