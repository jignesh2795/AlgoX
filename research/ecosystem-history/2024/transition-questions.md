# 2024 Transition Research Questions

## Primary question

Which 2024 projects and research results converted the experimental patterns of 2023 into reusable engineering capabilities?

## Priority tracks

1. Local inference and model runners
2. Open-weight models
3. RAG and retrieval infrastructure
4. Agent frameworks and bounded execution
5. Multimodal workflows
6. AI coding and repository-aware development
7. Evaluation and observability
8. Model serving and inference optimization
9. Workflow/UI composition
10. Memory and state management

## Required comparison

For each important 2024 project compare against the corresponding 2023 capability:

- What problem changed?
- What architectural boundary changed?
- What became standardized?
- What became commodity infrastructure?
- What failure mode was addressed?
- What remained unresolved?
- What capability is relevant to financial technology?

## Completion gate

Do not mark a 2024 capability as validated merely because the project became popular. Require source evidence and, where practical, reproduction or benchmark evidence.
