# 2025 Final Provisional Synthesis

Status: PROVISIONAL

## Executive conclusion

2025 research shifts the central problem from **building agents** to **engineering controllable learning systems**.

The durable architecture is no longer:

```text
Model → Prompt → Tool → Answer
```

It is:

```text
Experience
   ↓
Evidence
   ↓
Memory / Knowledge
   ↓
Context Compilation
   ↓
Task / Risk Classification
   ↓
Compute / Model / Tool Policy
   ↓
Agent Execution
   ↓
Verification
   ↓
Evaluation
   ↓
Decision
   ↓
Outcome
   ↓
Memory Consolidation
   ↺
```

## Major findings

### 1. The evaluator is part of the intelligence

Agent quality cannot be inferred from a final response. Tool calls, retrieved evidence, trajectory, verification, policy compliance and downstream outcomes must be evaluated.

### 2. Memory must be governed

Durable memory without provenance, temporal validity, contradiction handling and consolidation can preserve errors. Memory storage and institutional learning are separate capabilities.

### 3. Context is a compiled artifact

The model should not receive the entire institutional memory. AlgoX should compile bounded context from evidence, knowledge, temporal constraints, counter-evidence, policies and task requirements.

### 4. Compute is a policy decision

Reasoning depth, model selection, rollout count and verification budget should depend on task difficulty, risk, latency and cost rather than being permanently fixed.

### 5. Agent architecture is an experimental variable

Single-agent, planner/executor, multi-agent, memory-enabled and verifier-driven systems can behave differently. AlgoX must benchmark architecture as well as model.

### 6. Financial agents remain bounded

2025 financial research expands the use of agents in research, investment management, risk and trading, but live benchmarks show that general intelligence does not imply robust trading or risk management.

The safe research architecture remains:

```text
Agent
 ↓
Research / Analysis
 ↓
Recommendation
 ↓
Deterministic Policy Gate
 ↓
Risk Engine
 ↓
Execution Engine
 ↓
Venue
 ↓
Reconciliation
```

### 7. Live evidence is different from backtest evidence

AlgoX must preserve evaluation environment, data vintage, market regime, transaction-cost assumptions and execution model.

### 8. Observability, evaluation and audit are distinct

```text
Trace   → what happened?
Metric  → how much/how often?
Eval    → was it good/correct?
Audit   → what is the authoritative record?
```

They can share infrastructure but should not collapse into one semantic concept.

### 9. Multimodal observations belong in evidence

Charts, PDFs, screenshots, tables and other modalities should enter the same evidence/provenance lifecycle rather than becoming ephemeral prompt context.

### 10. PostgreSQL remains an engineering experiment, not a conclusion from hype

The 2025 evidence increases the need for durable transactions, traces, provenance and relational temporal state, but it does not justify changing the storage decision without benchmark evidence.

## 2025 strategic thesis

> **The next generation of AI systems will be differentiated less by whether they can reason, and more by whether they can accumulate evidence, evaluate their own behavior, preserve institutional memory, adapt under governance, and remain controllable at high-consequence boundaries.**

## What remains unresolved

- universal superiority of multi-agent systems
- whether memory consistently improves long-horizon financial decisions
- optimal adaptive compute policies
- reliable online evaluation without evaluation leakage
- robust multimodal financial reasoning
- continual learning without catastrophic or institutional-memory drift
- whether autonomous financial agents can satisfy production-grade risk and governance requirements

These remain explicit research questions rather than architecture assumptions.
