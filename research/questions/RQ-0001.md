# RQ-0001 — What makes a financial technology architecture robust?

## Question
What architectural and engineering patterns consistently improve reliability, correctness, extensibility, recovery, and operability in financial technology systems?

## Scope
Study open-source projects, academic research, exchange/broker documentation, production engineering literature, and relevant AI-era infrastructure.

## Target domains
Market data, execution, OMS/EMS, portfolio, risk, backtesting, storage, event systems, observability, security, and operations.

## Expected outputs
- Architecture comparison
- Capability inventory
- Reusable patterns
- Failure/anti-pattern catalog
- Experiments and benchmarks
- Candidate architecture decisions
